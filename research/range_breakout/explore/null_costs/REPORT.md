# Axis: null_costs

Configs tested: 366 (VAL checked: 8). Holdout (2024+) not used.

## Recommendation

No robust, cost-proof edge on this axis. The Asian-range breakout direction carries real in-sample information: +0.06R/trade over random direction, p about 0.01, mostly from downside-break continuation. In VAL the information is only +0.03R, p 0.28. The gross edge is about 0.5 USD/oz per trade, the same size as a retail round trip (0.47 USD). The default breaks even at 1.08x the modelled costs and at a 1.14x spread, which is inside the measured spread range on breakout bars, so it is not tradeable as is.

Rules for the synthesis stage:
(1) Require every candidate to beat its own random-direction null with null.null_test and to have m* >= 1.5, i.e. a break-even round trip of about 0.7 USD or more. Also check it at slip 0.10 with spread +0.10.
(2) Reject tight stops (< 0.75x range width or < 0.35 ATR) and narrow-range subsets. Cost in R scales with 1/stop, and the gross USD expectancy does not rise with a tighter stop.
(3) The only family with a real cost margin is the wide-range day filter (width/ATR >= 0.3-0.35, m* 2.3-2.8). Its net profitability fails a family-wise reality check even on a 32-config proxy family (p_family 0.06), so treat it as a hypothesis for VAL and holdout, not a finding.
(4) Use family_bootstrap over each axis's full searched family for the multiple-testing correction; family_null tests only direction information, not profit.
(5) For the EA, trade through a low-cost ECN route. Every 0.1 USD of round-trip cost is about 0.02R per trade at 1x Asian width.

## Key findings

- NULL, default Params(), IS: against a random-direction null at the same entry times, the breakout direction is significant. Actual +0.0078R vs coin-null mean -0.0562R (sd 0.0271), p=0.0098 with 10k permutations (0.015 with 200), shuffle null (same long/short count) p=0.0065, z=2.36. Direction information is +0.064R per trade. Always-long at the break times averages -0.0857R and always-short -0.0263R.
- NULL, default, VAL: not significant. Actual -0.0105R vs null mean -0.0425R (sd 0.055), p_coin=0.28, p_shuffle=0.25. Direction information is +0.032R, positive in both VAL years. Per IS year, direction information is positive in 6 of 8 years; net R is positive in only 4 of 8.
- The direction information sits in DOWNSIDE breaks. At down-break times the short beats a long entered at the same moment by +0.20R (t 2.57 IS; +0.22R, t 1.26 VAL). At up-breaks the long beats the short by only +0.06R (t 0.86 IS; -0.07R VAL). Gold's up-drift favours the long in both comparisons, so the down-break continuation is not drift.
- Random-time null (random bar in 07:00-12:00, random direction, same risk and exits): IS mean -0.071R, p=0.01; VAL p=0.18. Frictionless: IS null +0.019R vs actual +0.099R, p=0.005.
- COSTS, default: gross (frictionless) +0.0988R (t 2.88, L +0.084, S +0.114, 6/8 years) vs net +0.0078R. The cost drag is 0.091R per trade. In USD, gross is about +0.53 USD/oz per trade against a nominal round trip of 0.47 USD (0.07 commission + about 0.30 spread + 2 x 0.05 slip).
- BREAK-EVEN, default (IS): joint cost multiplier m*=1.08, i.e. a round trip of 0.51 USD. Exact break-even extra commission is +0.040 USD/oz. Break-even slip is 0.073 USD. Break-even spread multiplier is 1.14x, which falls inside the audit's measured actual/model spread range on breakout-like bars (0.97-1.19x). Stress (slip 0.10, spread +0.10): -0.032R. At half the default costs: +0.052R, t 1.53, still not significant.
- The gross USD expectancy per trade is nearly flat, about 0.45-0.56 USD, for any stop of 0.75x width or more, and it falls for tighter stops: 0.26 USD at 0.25x width, because early stop-outs cut off the continuation. Cost in R is roughly (0.07 + spread + 2 x slip) / stop distance: 0.09R at 1x width, 0.18R at 0.5x, 0.41R at 0.25x. Tight stops are therefore the most cost-fragile configs.
- Variants (IS gross -> net, m*): SL 0.5W + TP 2R: +0.086 -> -0.093R (t -2.9), m*=0.47, break-even round trip 0.21 USD, negative in every cost-grid cell. Fade + TP 1R: gross already negative (-0.051R, and worse than its random-direction null, p=0.99), net -0.109R. SL 2W: +0.045 -> -0.003R, m*=0.95. VAL at default costs: default -0.011, tight -0.143, fade -0.088, wide -0.021.
- Narrow-range days are cost-fragile. Default trades by width/ATR quintile: cost is 0.139R in the narrowest quintile (<0.25) and 0.049R in the widest (>0.47). min_w_atr=0.4 has gross +0.134, net +0.072 (t 1.59, 617 trades); max_w_atr=0.4 has gross +0.081, net -0.025.
- Year table (c): the spread (0.23-0.39 USD) is nearly flat while gold rose about 55%, so cost in R fell from about 0.10R (2014-19) to 0.06-0.07R (2020-23). The gross edge fell too: mean gross R about 0.14 in 2014-18 vs about 0.04 in 2019-23. The round-trip cost is 1.8-3.3% of ATR14.
- Other axes' finalists (IS only, not selection-corrected): every breakout-family finalist has p_coin <= 0.008, so they share the same direction information. What differs is the cost margin. The exits F1-F3 and entry B finalists have m* 1.13-1.22 (break-even round trip 0.53-0.57 USD, all negative under stress). The timing finalists have m* 1.34-1.56. The wide-range filter finalists (min_w_atr 0.3-0.35, optionally with ATR14/mean20<=1) have m* 2.3-2.8 (break-even round trip 1.07-1.34 USD) and stay positive under stress (+0.05 to +0.10R).
- Family-wise tests on a 32-config proxy of the filters search (min_w_atr x ATR-regime cap, IS). Direction information survives max-z correction: best p_family 0.003. Net profitability does not: stationary-bootstrap reality check max-t, best config (min_w_atr=0.35 & a20<=1, +0.139R, t 2.55) p_family=0.061, next 0.13. The real filter search was larger than 32 configs, so the true adjusted p-values are higher. Default alone: bootstrap p=0.39.
- null.py reusable API: null_test(params, n_perm, trades=...), null_time_test, family_null (direction, max-z with shared coin per date), family_bootstrap (net-profit reality check). The re-walker reproduces engine.run bitwise on 18 different Params plus a spread x2 dataset. The entry-bar SL rule does not bias the comparison (0 actual vs 3 flipped entry-bar stop-outs of 1828).

The default's break-even spread multiplier (1.14x) falls inside the audit's measured actual/model ratio on breakout-like bars (0.97-1.19x). Backtest results for high-frequency, narrow-stop configs are therefore optimistic.
