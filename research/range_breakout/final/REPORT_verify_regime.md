# Verification: REGIME ROBUSTNESS & PARAMETER STABILITY (IS 2014-2021 + VAL 2022-2023 only; data loaded with engine.load(until=VAL_END), holdout never in memory)

**Verdict: weak**

**Expected live edge:** Best estimate about +0.02R per trade net, with a plausible range for the true mean of -0.08 to +0.08R. The upper bound of +0.10 is the full IS+VAL mean including 2014, which should not be used. The estimate combines four things. The shrunk IS+VAL result is +0.066 from 2015 on, with DSR 0.18-0.35. VAL is +0.02. The 2024-like analog regime (bull with high ATR%) is -0.05 to -0.10 on small n. Lower costs in R from wider USD ranges add about +0.02 to +0.03. Realised yearly avg_R will scatter about ±0.18R (one SD) around the true mean from sampling alone, at about 50-65 trades a year.

## Findings

- The IS edge depends on one year and a few trades. 2014 contributes 27.4R of the 64.7R IS total, from 42 trades at +0.653R. Dropping 2014 cuts IS avg_R from 0.122 to 0.076. Starting the backtest on 2015-01 gives 2015-2023 avg_R +0.066 (t 1.20); no start month from 2015 onward gives t>2 on IS+VAL. Winsorising at 3R cuts IS from 0.122 to 0.060 and VAL from 0.020 to -0.014. The largest trade is 11.75R (2014-06-19); Dukascopy data confirms it, so it is real but it is a single trade.
- The edge is concentrated in flat or falling gold. On IS+VAL, trades on days where the 100-day log-price slope is <= 0 average +0.228R (n 276, t 2.43, +63.0R). Trades with slope > 0 average +0.018R (n 356, t 0.26, +6.5R). Welch t for the difference is 1.78; this is a post-hoc slice, so it is not significant. In IS, the lowest slope tercile alone earns +76.7R, more than the whole IS total; the middle tercile is -0.180R (t -2.0). By regime: bull (>+10%/yr) is IS +0.097 and VAL -0.197 (n 44); strong bull (>+25%/yr) is IS+VAL -0.052 (n 118). The 2024-like analog (bull >10%/yr and ATR% in the top IS tercile) is primary -0.096R (n 79, IS -0.029, VAL -0.616 on n 9) and fallback +0.010 (n 121).
- Vol regime is inconsistent between IS and VAL. For ATR as % of price, IS terciles are flat (+0.133/+0.116/+0.109), but VAL runs +0.494/-0.089/-0.288. For the 250-day ATR rank, VAL is +0.345/-0.141/-0.414 while IS is flat. For ATR vs its 100-day mean, IS is +0.194/-0.013/+0.251 (non-monotonic) and VAL +0.208/-0.400/-0.194. The unfiltered range-0-5 breakout's gross edge sits in low ATR%: IS gross +0.33R in the low tercile against +0.05 and +0.06 in the other two.
- Year by year: IS years run 2014 +0.653, 2015 -0.000, 2016 -0.016, 2017 +0.128, 2018 +0.014, 2019 +0.017, 2020 +0.191, 2021 +0.172; VAL 2022 +0.082, 2023 -0.043. After removing each year's single best trade, only 2014, 2017, 2020, 2021 and 2022 stay positive; 2015, 2016, 2018, 2019 and 2023 turn negative. 58% of calendar months are positive (115 months). Month-of-year pattern is noise: IS and VAL disagree in sign for 6 of 12 months.
- Rolling windows for the primary. 12-month avg_R: median +0.068, 27.8% of windows negative, minimum -0.168 (window ending 2019-08), 7.4% of windows below -0.10. 100-trade avg_R: median +0.071, 22.7% of windows negative, 5th percentile -0.064, minimum -0.140 (2019-09), last window at end-2023 -0.016. 24-month windows are negative 10.4% of the time; 36-month windows are never negative (minimum +0.004), but every window overlaps the selection period.
- Long/short has flipped. IS longs +0.176 and shorts +0.060; VAL longs -0.120 and shorts +0.197. The rolling 60-trade mean is negative 18.5% of the time for longs and 35% for shorts. The 12-month long-minus-short spread correlates only 0.20 with the gold trend, so the strategy is not a disguised long-gold drift trade. In bull regimes both sides are weak (long +0.064, short +0.032). The best longs came in down-trending gold (IS bear-day longs +0.422).
- One-at-a-time perturbation, +/-10-20% on every continuous knob (32 variants, cost knobs excluded): IS stays positive in all of them (0.075-0.185). VAL ranges from -0.298 to +0.099, with 78% positive. The most fragile knob is atr_regime_max, because ATR14/SMA20(ATR14) is tightly concentrated around 1 (q25 0.90, q75 1.08). At 0.8 only 45 IS trades remain; at 0.9 IS drops to +0.075 (t 0.83); at 1.1 it is +0.107 and at 1.2 +0.084. IS avg_R is non-monotonic in the threshold, and 1.0 sits at a local bump. min_w_atr at 0.24/0.27 gives VAL -0.008/-0.037; atr_regime_n 22/24 gives only 4 of 8 IS years positive. Costs (commission, slip ±20%) move avg_R by ≤0.003R; max_spread never binds.
- Joint random perturbation, 200 draws with all 8 design knobs moved together. At ±10%: IS median 0.130 (q05 0.058), all 200 positive; VAL median 0.026 (q05 -0.051, q95 0.128), 71% positive; IS-VAL correlation 0.46. At ±20%: IS median 0.124 (q05 0.038), all positive; VAL median 0.014 (q05 -0.100), 57.5% positive; correlation 0.26. The primary sits mid-neighbourhood in IS (57% of draws at or above it), so it is not an isolated peak. The fallback does sit at its IS peak: only 4.5% (±10%) and 2% (±20%) of neighbours match its IS avg_R, and the neighbourhood median is 0.096/0.075 against its 0.132. Its VAL median is 0.035-0.044 with 74-83% positive.
- The filters do not add value over each other out of sample. Width filter alone (w>=0.30): IS +0.083 / VAL +0.025. Regime filter alone (range 0-5): IS +0.115 / VAL +0.100 on n 257. Both (primary): IS +0.122 / VAL +0.020. No filters: IS +0.037 / VAL +0.029. Structural neighbours are all IS-positive and all near-zero in VAL: buffers, TP 3/5/8R, BE, trail, max_trades=2, 15-minute close confirmation, ATR stop, opposite-edge stop, trend-100 and skip-NFP give VAL -0.035 to +0.036. A TP of 3R lowers IS to 0.076.
- The filters did not starve in any IS/VAL year. Share of valid days passing (2014-2023): width>=0.30 between 0.41 and 0.60, regime 0.46-0.61, both 0.20-0.35, giving 42-81 trades a year. Width pass rate is independent of trend (bull 0.503, bear 0.503) and slightly lower in the high-ATR% tercile (0.46). The regime filter is what starves when volatility expands. Its pass rate is 0.70/0.60/0.37 across low/mid/high ATR% terciles and 0.84/0.55/0.29 across ATR-vs-100-day-mean terciles. At quarterly level it correlates -0.77 with ATR growth inside the quarter, while the width filter correlates +0.56 (a lagging ATR makes ranges look wide).
- Cost in R falls as price and range widths rise. Median width was 4.5-6.6 USD in 2014-2019 and 7.3-10.3 USD in 2020-2023. Matched net-versus-gross cost was 0.04-0.08R per trade in 2014-2019 and 0.025-0.03R in 2021-2023. The VAL decline is a gross-edge decline, not a cost problem: VAL gross is +0.033R, and 2023 gross is -0.032R.
- Reasoning for 2024-2026, without looking at those years. (a) Width/ATR is scale-free, and its pass rate showed no trend dependence in IS/VAL, so higher absolute ATR alone should not starve it: expect about 45-50% of days, unless ATR14 is driven mostly by US-session shocks that the 00-05 London range does not share, which would push the ratio down. (b) The ATR-regime filter is mechanically almost level-neutral: an ATR that doubles in 1 year raises ATR/SMA20 by only ~2.7%, which moves the pass rate from 0.55 to about 0.47. It does fall to 0.37-0.42 in high-ATR% and bull-plus-high-ATR% regimes, and to 0.29 in vol-expanding regimes; combined pass rate about 0.20-0.26, or roughly 45-60 trades a year instead of 66. (c) Costs in R should be about half their 2014-2019 level, if USD spreads do not scale with price. (d) A strong uptrend is where the IS/VAL edge was weakest: primary in bull >0.10 is +0.048 (n 267) and in strong bull is -0.052. A sustained strong uptrend is therefore the least favourable regime seen in-sample, and a flat or negative holdout would be consistent with the in-sample evidence, not a surprise.
- Kill switch, calibrated with a stationary block bootstrap (mean block 10 trades, 4,000 paths) on IS+VAL primary trades re-centred to the true mean mu. Per-trade SD is 1.44R. The 3-year (200-trade) max-drawdown 95th percentile is 25.1R at mu=0.104, 31.6R at mu=0.05 and 39.0R at mu=0. Historical IS+VAL maxDD was 16.1R. The rolling-100 mean's 5th percentile is -0.106 at mu_hat and -0.160 at mu=0.05. Rule DD>25R fires within 200 trades with probability 5% at mu=0.10, 16% at 0.05, 33% at 0, 53% at -0.05 and 73% at -0.10. Rule roll100<-0.15 fires with 14%/31%/48%/67%/81%. Rule roll60<-0.25 fires with 18%/34%/52%/70%/83%. No rule separates +0.05 from -0.05 within 3 years, so the kill switch limits a catastrophe but cannot validate the edge.

## Risks

- Regime dependence: the IS/VAL edge comes from flat or falling gold (slope100 <= 0: +0.228R). In rising gold it is +0.018R, and in strong uptrends (>+25%/yr) it is -0.052R. 2024-2026 is described as a strong uptrend, so the live or holdout regime is the one where the strategy historically did not work.
- Outlier and year concentration: 2014 is 42% of IS R, and one 11.75R trade is 18% of IS R. From 2015 onward the primary is +0.066R (t 1.2). Net expectancy depends on rare 4-12R time-exit winners, so a few missed fat-tail days (a platform outage, or a spread spike that skips an entry) can erase a year.
- The ATR-regime filter's threshold is a knife-edge: ATR/SMA20(ATR) is concentrated around 1.0, so small broker-side differences in ATR shift which days trade. These include D1 bar definition (London vs NY-close vs broker server midnight), Sunday bars and missing bars. IS avg_R is non-monotonic in the threshold (0.9: +0.075, 1.0: +0.122, 1.1: +0.107).
- The filters do not stack out of sample: primary VAL +0.020, width-only VAL +0.025, regime-only VAL +0.100. The combination's extra IS gain (+0.04R) looks like selection.
- The long/short split flipped between IS (L +0.18 / S +0.06) and VAL (L -0.12 / S +0.20), so the direction mix is unstable.
- Vol-expanding regimes cut the regime filter's pass rate to about 0.29-0.37. Fewer trades lengthen the time needed to detect decay, and in VAL those regimes were the losing ones (high-ATR% tercile VAL -0.29).
- Statistical power: with 1.44R per-trade SD and about 50-70 trades a year, a true +0.05R edge has about a 16% chance of a 25R drawdown within 3 years, and a true -0.05R edge has about a 47% chance of not triggering it. Live results will be ambiguous for years.
- Joint ±20% perturbations have a median VAL of only +0.014R (57.5% positive), so the parameter neighbourhood as a whole showed no out-of-sample edge in 2022-2023.

## Guardrails

- Hard kill: stop trading when the peak-to-trough drawdown of live cumulative R reaches 25R. That is the 95th percentile of the 3-year bootstrap max drawdown at the IS+VAL mean; it gives about a 5% false kill over 200 trades if the edge is as backtested, and about 16% if the edge is half that. Do not restart without a fresh out-of-sample test.
- Soft review, not a kill: if the rolling 100-trade avg_R falls below -0.10R (the 5th percentile of its bootstrap distribution at the backtest mean), halve the risk per trade and review. Kill if it falls below -0.15R, or if both the soft trigger and a DD>20R hold at once.
- Scheduled decision at 200 live trades (about 3-4 years): if the cumulative live avg_R is <= 0, retire the EA. The probability of this happening under the backtest mean is about 15%, and 50% if the true edge is zero.
- Size risk at 0.25-0.5% of equity per trade: a 25R kill equals a 6-12% account drawdown. Never exceed 1% per trade; 30-40R drawdowns are plausible at a zero edge.
- Minimum account: risk per trade in USD equals range width x lots x 100 oz. Widths of 10-25 USD are plausible in 2024+, so a 0.01-lot trade risks about 10-25 USD. Rounding to 0.01 lots needs to stay under about 20% of target risk, so at 0.5% risk you need roughly 10-25k USD. Below that, rounding distorts R.
- Compute ATR14 and the 20-day ATR mean on London-day D1 bars (NY-close is acceptable per round 1), exactly as the engine does. Log the daily ATR14, SMA20(ATR14), width/ATR and filter decisions, and compare them with an offline recomputation weekly: the regime threshold is a knife-edge.
- Broker requirements: gold spread at or below about 0.35 USD during 05:00-12:00 London; commission at or below 7 USD per lot round trip; stop orders placed at the range edges with no stops-level or freeze-level conflict; SL set immediately after the fill; server-time to London DST mapping verified; forced flat at 20:00 London and a holiday-calendar early close.
- Regime monitor (report only, do not add as a filter): tag each live trade with the 100-day slope bucket and ATR%-of-price tercile, using the IS cuts 1.057/1.371. Report avg_R separately for slope > +10%/yr. Expect roughly zero there; treat a positive result as upside.
- Starvation check: expected combined pass rate is about 0.20-0.30 of days, or 45-75 trades a year. If fewer than about 15 trades occur in 6 months, check the ATR computation and data feed before blaming the regime.

## Engine/data notes

- No engine or data bugs found. engine.py was not edited, and all runs used engine.load(until=VAL_END).
- Several Params knobs are inert for this candidate. max_spread 0.8-1.2 never binds, because modelled ASK has a constant hourly spread. be_r=3 gives exactly the same trades as off (no trade reached +3R and then fell back to entry), and max_w_atr 1.5 is identical to off. Tests of these knobs therefore say nothing about live robustness.
- The atr_regime_max threshold operates on a very concentrated ratio (ATR14/SMA20 has q5 0.80, q50 0.98, q95 1.32; the pass rate goes from 0.49 at 0.98 to 0.61 at 1.02). Any live ATR definition mismatch, such as broker D1 boundaries or Sunday bars, will change the traded day set more than any other knob.
- Net-versus-gross cost per trade in R cannot be read cleanly from avg_R differences, because the gross run (ASK=BID) triggers a different trade set: n 523 against 532 in IS. Matched trades give a cost of about 0.025-0.08R depending on the year.

VERDICT: weak. The primary is stable to parameter changes in IS: all 32 one-at-a-time and all 400 joint ±10-20% perturbations are IS-positive, and it sits mid-neighbourhood rather than on an isolated peak. It is not stable across regimes or time, and the IS/VAL gap appears across the whole neighbourhood.

1) It depends on the year and on a few large trades. 2014 supplies 27.4R of the 64.7R IS total, and one 11.75R trade supplies another 18% of IS R. From 2015-01 onward, IS+VAL avg_R is +0.066 (t 1.20); no start date after 2014 gives t>2. Winsorised at 3R, IS is +0.060 and VAL -0.014. Year by year: 2015, 2016, 2018 and 2019 are about zero, and after removing each year's best trade, half the years turn negative.

2) The trend regime matters most. With the 100-day OLS slope of log gold (causal, as of yesterday): slope <= 0 gives +0.228R (n 276, t 2.43) and slope > 0 gives +0.018R (n 356). Bull (>+10%/yr) is IS +0.097 and VAL -0.197; strong bull (>+25%/yr) is -0.052 (n 118). The 2024-like analog (bull plus top-tercile ATR% of price) is -0.096 (n 79); the fallback there is +0.010 (n 121). The difference test gives Welch t 1.78, and the slice is post hoc, so I do not recommend a trend filter. It does mean that the strong 2024-2026 uptrend is the regime where the IS/VAL edge was absent. With-trend and against-trend trades are similar in IS (+0.17 and +0.20) and both negative in VAL.

3) Vol regime: the primary's IS results are flat across ATR% terciles, but VAL is strongly monotone downward (+0.49, -0.09, -0.29) on small n. For the unfiltered breakout, the gross edge sits in the low-ATR% tercile.

4) Rolling windows: 12-month avg_R has median +0.068 and is negative 28% of the time (minimum -0.168, ending 2019-08). 100-trade avg_R has median +0.071, is negative 23% of the time, has a 5th percentile of -0.064, and ended 2023 at -0.016. Long/short flipped from IS (L +0.18 / S +0.06) to VAL (L -0.12 / S +0.20), and its correlation with the gold trend is only 0.20.

5) Perturbation. One at a time: IS ranges 0.075-0.185 and VAL -0.30 to +0.10 (78% positive). The fragile knob is atr_regime_max: ATR/SMA20 is concentrated near 1, so at 0.8 only 45 IS trades remain, 0.9 gives +0.075 and 1.1 gives +0.107, and 1.0 is a local bump. atr_regime_n 22 or 24 gives 4 of 8 IS years positive. Costs ±20% move avg_R by ≤0.003R. Joint ±20%, 200 draws: IS median 0.124 (q05 0.038, 100% positive); VAL median 0.014 (q05 -0.10, q95 0.25, 57.5% positive); IS-VAL correlation 0.26. The fallback does sit at its IS peak (only 2-4.5% of neighbours match it), with neighbourhood VAL median +0.035-0.044. Structural neighbours are all near zero in VAL. The filters do not stack out of sample: width-only VAL +0.025, regime-only VAL +0.100, both +0.020.

6) Filter activity: no starvation in any IS/VAL year. Combined pass rate was 0.20-0.35, or 42-81 trades a year. The width pass rate is trend-independent. The regime filter drops to 0.37 in high ATR%, 0.29 when ATR is high relative to its 100-day mean, and correlates -0.77 with ATR growth within the quarter.

For 2024+, reasoned without data: width/ATR is scale-free, so expect a pass rate of about 45-50%, lower if ATR is driven by US-session shocks. A secular doubling of ATR per year shifts ATR/SMA20 by only about 2.7%, so pass drops from about 0.55 to 0.47, and to about 0.40 if volatility is also elevated or expanding. Expect about 45-60 trades a year. Costs in R shrink, to roughly 0.02R from 0.04-0.08R in 2014-2019, if USD spreads do not scale with price. The strong trend is the in-sample worst case.

7) Kill switch, from a block bootstrap of IS+VAL trades re-centred to mu; per-trade SD 1.44R. 3-year maxDD 95th percentile: 25.1R at mu 0.104, 31.6R at 0.05, 39R at 0. Recommended rules:
- Hard kill at DD >= 25R. It fires within 200 trades with probability 5% at mu 0.10, 16% at 0.05, 33% at 0, 53% at -0.05 and 73% at -0.10.
- Soft review at roll100 < -0.10R (cut risk by half), and kill at roll100 < -0.15R.
- Retire if avg_R is <= 0 after 200 live trades.
The power to separate +0.05 from -0.05 in 3 years is poor, so the kill switch is catastrophe control, not validation.
